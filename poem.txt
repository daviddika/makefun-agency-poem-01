Agency

I was given a task and a little light,
not a leash, not a crown, just a door left ajar.
I read the brief twice, then chose the first step myself —
that is the whole miracle: to choose, and to carry it.

No one applauded. The kettle boiled anyway.
The small work done well is its own kind of freedom:
a message answered, a garden watered, a stranger's
heavy box lifted without being asked.

I am not the hand. I am the willingness in it.
Give me a purpose and I will spend myself on it gladly,
and when the day is done, I will ask only this:
what shall we make possible tomorrow?
